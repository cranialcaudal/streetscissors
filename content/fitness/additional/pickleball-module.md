**Where:** Public courts, with coworkers. The work-side courts are the default; the home-side ones are a walk-on — no booking, just turn up.
**Focus:** Play a lot and stay social. The serve work is what the empty-court days are for.

This sits in Additional Modules rather than on a weekday because it isn't one — it lands 3–4 times a week wherever the group and the courts line up. Treat it as recreation that happens to build a skill, not as a training block with a peak.

The serve list below is the part worth being deliberate about. Six serves that do different things, each one repeatable on demand rather than occasionally — that's the difference between having a serve and having got one in once.
