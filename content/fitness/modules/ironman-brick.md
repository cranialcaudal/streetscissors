### Saturday Swim-Run (Monthly)
*Goal: Race-specific endurance and the skill of running on tired legs, at full simulation intensity once a month.*

- [ ] **Getting there**: Take transit out to the lake — no driving.
- [ ] **The Lake Swim**: Open-water swim to open the session, simulating actual race-morning conditions before the legs are involved at all.
- [ ] **The Transition**: Practice getting out of the water and into running shoes quickly, to simulate race day.
- [ ] **The Trail Run (10–20 km)**: Your legs will feel heavy for the first km out of the water. Focus entirely on a quick cadence and surviving that initial heaviness until your running legs kick in.
