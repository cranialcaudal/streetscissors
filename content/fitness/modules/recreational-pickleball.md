### Pickleball — 3–4× a week
*Goal: Play a lot, stay social, and use the empty-court days to build a real serve arsenal. Recreation first — this is not a training block with a peak.*

- [ ] **Find a Court**: Public courts, with coworkers. The work-side courts are the default; the home-side ones are a walk-on — no booking, just turn up and get in the next rotation.
- [ ] **Play 2–9 Games**: However many the group has in it. Play them, then go home — there's no target number and no reason to stretch it out.
- [ ] **Watch the Joints**: Lateral cutting and stop-start sprints are where the ankle and knee risk lives. If the legs are already cooked from a lift or a long run, dial the intensity back rather than skipping the session.
- [ ] **If Nobody's There**: The session becomes serve practice. Take the serve list below and work it.

### Serve Practice — When the Courts Are Empty
*Goal: Six serves that do different things, each one repeatable on demand rather than occasionally.*

- [ ] **Topspin**: The workhorse. Heavy forward rotation, dips late and kicks up off the bounce. This is the one to groove first — everything else is a variation off a reliable topspin motion.
- [ ] **Square-on-T**: Aimed at the T, hit square rather than angled. Takes away the returner's angle and pulls them to the middle.
- [ ] **Back of the Line (Deep)**: Depth as the weapon. Land it near the baseline to push the return back and buy time to get to the kitchen line.
- [ ] **Screwball**: Sidespin that breaks the opposite way to what the motion advertises. Effective precisely because it looks like the topspin until it bounces.
- [ ] **Center-Box**: Placed into the middle of the service box rather than to a corner. The safe, high-percentage option — the one to fall back on when the others are missing.
- [ ] **Dragon**: The heavy-spin trick serve. Lowest percentage of the six, so save it rather than lead with it.

- [ ] **Serve Blocks, Not Reps**: Ten to fifteen of one serve in a row before switching. Alternating every ball trains nothing.
- [ ] **Call the Target First**: Name the serve and the spot out loud before hitting. A serve you can only produce by accident isn't in the arsenal yet.
