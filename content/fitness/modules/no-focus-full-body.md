### No Focus — Full Body (80 Minutes)
*Goal: Single-leg drive and shoulder refinement for boxing/baseball, layered onto general athletic full-body work. Runs twice a week — before work on Monday (first, while fresh, ahead of the pre-work swim), and at the gym on Saturday's Gym + Hike weeks.*

**Warm-Up (~10 min):** Light cardio, dynamic hip openers, band pull-aparts, and a few empty/light warm-up sets on the first two lifts.

### Block 1 — Heavy Lower (straight sets, ~90 sec rest)

- [ ] **[[hex-flat-side-deadlift|Hex Flat-Side Deadlifts]]**: 3 x 6–8 — 2x45 lbs
- [ ] **[[bulgarian-split-squat|Bulgarian Split Squats]]**: 3 x 8–10 per leg — 25 lb kettlebell goblet-style (or 30–45 lb plate)
    - Single-leg force production and hip stability.
    - Elevate the front foot slightly for more quad bias.

### Block 2 — Athletic Pressing (straight sets, ~90 sec rest)

- [ ] **[[landmine-press|Athletic Staggered-Stance Landmine Press]]**: 3 x 8–10 — 30 lbs
- [ ] **[[landmine-rotational-press|Landmine Rotational Press]]** *(boxing/baseball kinetic chain)*: 3 x 8 per side — same 30 lb landmine setup
    - Hip-to-shoulder force transfer: the movement pattern of a punch and a swing.
    - Drive from the rear hip; let the press finish the rotation.

### Block 3 — Superset A (~60 sec rest between rounds)

- [ ] **[[lateral-raises|Cable Lateral Raises]]**: 3 x 15–20 — 10 lbs
- [ ] **[[kettlebell-russian-twist|Kettlebell Russian Twists with Leg Out-Flares]]**: 3 x 14 — 25 lbs

### Block 4 — Superset B (~60 sec rest between rounds)

- [ ] **[[bench-tricep-dips|Bench Tricep Dips]]**: 3 x 12–15
- [ ] **[[face-pulls|Face Pulls]]** *(shoulder health for throwing/punching)*: 3 x 15–20 — cable, ~20–30 lbs
    - Rear delts, external rotators, mid-traps.
    - Balances all the pressing; protects the rotator cuff for baseball and speeds punch retraction.

**Time budget:** Warm-up ~10 min, Block 1 ~15 min, Block 2 ~14 min, Block 3 ~9 min, Block 4 ~9 min, transitions/buffer ~13 min — **~80 min total**.

> Order logic: heavy hinge and single-leg work first while fresh, then athletic pressing, then isolation/core supersets last. Progression: once you hit the top of a rep range across all sets, add weight next session. The superset pairings don't compete muscularly, so quality shouldn't drop.
