### Remote Friday — Fartlek Run
*Goal: A genuine fast gear on the weeks there's no pool, without touching the legs you need for Saturday.*

- [ ] **Easy Build (10–15 min)**: Start at a conversational pace and let the legs open up on their own. No surges until the breathing has settled.
- [ ] **Fartlek Block (20–30 min)**: Speed play by feel, not a stopwatch. Surge to a landmark — a corner, a tree, the end of a block — then jog easy until you've genuinely recovered. Vary the length of the surges deliberately; the point is unstructured, not random.
- [ ] **Control the Ceiling**: Surges are strong, not all-out. If a surge leaves you needing more than a couple of minutes to recover, it was too hard for this session.
- [ ] **Easy Down (10 min)**: Back to conversational. Finish feeling like you could do the whole thing again.
- [ ] **[[couch-stretch|Couch Stretch]] (2 mins/side)**: The hip flexors take the brunt of repeated accelerations.
