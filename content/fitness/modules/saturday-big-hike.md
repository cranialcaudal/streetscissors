### Saturday — Big Hike (Solo)
*Goal: Long, unhurried time on the feet. Solo, no gym, no clock.*

- [ ] **Get Out There**: Take transit out — the trailhead is the start of the session, not a stage of it.
- [ ] **The Hike (3–5 hrs)**: Steady, sustainable effort with real vertical. Walk the climbs, don't race them. This is aerobic base built at a pace you could hold all day.
- [ ] **Fuel On The Move**: Eat and drink on schedule rather than when you notice you need it. Same habit the long course demands.
- [ ] **Descend Under Control**: The downhills are where the legs actually get wrecked. Short steps, quiet feet — you have a game tomorrow.
- [ ] **[[couch-stretch|Couch Stretch]] (2 mins/side)**: Hip flexors and quads after hours of climbing.
