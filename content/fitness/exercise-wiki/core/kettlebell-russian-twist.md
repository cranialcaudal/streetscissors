**Goal:** Rotational core strength combined with a hip-flexor/leg-lever component, for sport-specific anti-rotation and rotational power.

### Execution
Sit on the floor with knees bent and feet elevated off the ground, holding a kettlebell at the chest. Rotate the torso from side to side, tapping the bell down near the hip on each side. On each rep, flare the extended legs outward and back in, adding a leg-lever movement on top of the torso rotation. Rotate from the actual torso rather than just swinging the arms, and keep the lower back from rounding.

### Why it works (theory)
Standard Russian twists isolate torso rotation; adding the leg out-flare layers a hip-flexor/lower-ab demand onto the same rep, so the exercise trains rotation and hip-flexor control together rather than as separate movements. This is a programming rationale, not a cited claim.

### Programming notes
3 x 14 (7 per side). If the lower back rounds or the feet touch down before the set is finished, reduce the load or keep the feet slightly lower to make the lever shorter.
