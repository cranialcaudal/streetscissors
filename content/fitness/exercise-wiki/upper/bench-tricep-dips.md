**Goal:** Triceps isolation with less shoulder/chest involvement than a parallel-bar dip.

### Execution
Hands on the edge of a bench behind you, fingers pointing forward, legs extended out in front with feet on the floor (or another bench to increase difficulty). Lower the hips toward the floor by bending the elbows, then press back up to full extension. Keep the shoulders packed down away from the ears throughout — don't let them creep up toward the ears at the bottom.

### Why it works (theory)
The fixed hand position and extended-leg setup keep the torso more upright and the elbow path narrower than a parallel-bar dip, which is generally thought to shift more of the load onto the triceps and less onto the chest and shoulders. This is a mechanical inference from the setup, not a cited claim.

### Programming notes
3 x 12-15. If the shoulders round forward or pinch at the bottom, shorten the range of motion rather than pushing through pain.
