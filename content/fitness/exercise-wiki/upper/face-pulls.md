**Goal:** Balance out heavy pressing volume and support rotator-cuff and scapular health for throwing and punching.

### Execution
Set a cable at upper-chest to head height with a rope attachment. Pull the rope toward the face, leading with the elbows high, and pull the hands apart at the end of the movement to add external rotation at the shoulder. Control the return rather than letting the weight stack yank the arms back forward.

### Why it works (theory)
Pressing-heavy programs tend to under-train the muscles on the back of the shoulder (rear delts, external rotators, mid-traps) relative to the front. Those same muscles are the ones responsible for decelerating a throwing or punching arm, so keeping them strong is generally considered protective for the shoulder in throwing/striking sports. This is a widely held training rationale, not a specific cited finding for this exercise.

### Programming notes
3 x 15-20, light-to-moderate weight, strict form. This is a health/balance exercise, not a max-effort one — if you need to swing the weight to move it, it's too heavy.
