**Where:** Rotates on a repeating 4-week cycle — see below.
**Focus:** Full simulation intensity once a month, strength and connection twice a month, solo endurance once a month.

Saturday rotates through four different jobs on a repeating four-week cycle. Whichever one lands this week, recover heavily afterward — tomorrow is Sunday's game.

**Week 1 — Big Hike (solo):** Out to the trailhead and hike alone. No gym, no company — the hike is the whole day.

**Week 2 — Gym + Hike:** No Focus – Full Body at the gym with a partner (the same 80-minute session from Monday — see below), then a hike together. This is also the week's Saturday swim.

**Week 3 — Swim-Run:** The full simulation — see the block below. Transit out to the lake, open-water swim, transition, then the trail run. This only happens once a month now, not every other week, so treat it as the month's one full-intensity dress rehearsal.

**Week 4 — Gym + Hike:** Same as Week 2.
