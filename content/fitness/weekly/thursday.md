**Where:** Gym, before work. ~80 min plus a shower/change buffer.
**Focus:** Dedicated leg strength without Olympic lifting complexity. 

You already have a long run behind you for the week. Today is focused on raw neural strength rather than exhausting high-rep burnouts. This will prep you to lift a 150lb person without breaking your back.

After work is your second court/field evening — pickleball (see the Pickleball module), or a league game when it's on the calendar. If it's a game, the throwing counts as arm load: keep the volume sane so you're not arriving at Sunday's pitching outing with an already-taxed shoulder.
