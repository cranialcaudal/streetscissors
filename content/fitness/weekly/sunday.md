**Where:** Baseball diamond. Game times vary week to week.
**Focus:** Competing and staying healthy.

Sunday is purely about performance. Because you pitched today (or played the outfield), you must ensure that your arm is taken care of. The 60-minute pre-game warmup below is specifically engineered to protect your UCL and shoulder capsule before you even touch a baseball — start it an hour before whatever slot the game landed on that week.

Light tennis can land either before or after the game. If it's before, count it as a real shoulder cost — serves and overheads tax the same rotator cuff you're about to pitch with. On those days keep it light, skip max serving, and treat the warmup below as non-negotiable regardless. If tennis is after, it's a lighter concern: the pitching workload is already done, so it's just added fatigue on an arm that's already worked, not a pre-fatigue risk — but it's still not the place for hard hitting on a tired arm.

After the game, go home and rest. Tomorrow is Monday — recovery from today and yesterday, though not from all training load anymore.
