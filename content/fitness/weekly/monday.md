**Where:** Gym, then the pool across the way — both before the workday. ~80 min lift, ~90 min swim, plus transit either side.
**Focus:** A real strength session while fresh, then the Ironman aerobic engine — both done before the workday starts, same as Tuesday and Thursday's gym mornings.

No Focus – Full Body comes first, while you're fresh — heavy hinge and single-leg work is the whole point of that session, and it belongs before fatigue sets in, not after 90 minutes of swimming. From there it's a short walk to the pool: ninety minutes of smooth, aerobic freestyle builds the diesel engine that carries a 2.4-mile swim without spiking fatigue into the rest of the week. This is the *easier* of your two swim days — it stays at easy, controlled effort even coming after the lift.

It is an early alarm — two full sessions before the workday is a long morning — but front-loading them is what makes the rest of the week fit.

After work: pickleball, then home. Two court days a week (here and Thursday) is plenty of lateral, stop-start load on the knees and ankles — keep it social, not intervals.

> The throwing shoulder is one day removed from Sunday's pitch. Keep the lift's pressing work conservative and swim kindly — long and quiet, not thrashy — leaning on the kick and backstroke sets if it's still tender. Save the hard, fast, survival work for Friday.
