**Where:** Home. No fixed departure time — remote days have no arrival to hit.
**Focus:** Building the aerobic engine for 140.6 miles.

Working from home means zero commute fatigue. Fresh off Monday's rest and Tuesday's upper-body day, this is the safest place in the week for your true Ironman long run.

The run itself starts in the 5–10 km range and grows from there — the distance and time will keep scaling up aggressively as race day approaches, same as always.
