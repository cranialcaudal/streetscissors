# 2023), from the Discalced Carmelite Liturgical Calendar (USA), revised

# entry with the same identifier in the calendars it extends, wherever that
# one is dated: that is how the Order raises a rank (Saint Teresa) and how it
# moves a saint out of the way of its own (Saint Henry to July 10).
